Sports are how we show up for kids.

A team gives a kid somewhere to be after school, a coach who expects something of them, and friends working toward the same goal. That's what we build at Central.

  • Open to every skill level. First-timers and future varsity players train side by side.
  • Built around character. Effort, respect, and grades matter as much as the scoreboard.
  • Rooted in the East Valley. Local coaches, local gyms, local families.

Our programs

Two sports we know inside and out, plus support for any sport your kid wants to chase.

Basketball

Skills clinics, league teams, and open gym. Players build fundamentals, court sense, and unselfish team play.

Meet Reggie

Founder and lead coach

Reggie started Central with a simple belief: the right coach at the right time can change the direction of a kid's life. Sports gave him structure, mentors, and a path forward, and he wants every young athlete in Mesa and Gilbert to have the same.

He leads Central's basketball and wrestling programs, recruits volunteer coaches, and works with families one by one to get kids into the sports they love.
